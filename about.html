About Unclaimed Money Finder

Welcome to Unclaimed Money Finder, a free public service dedicated to helping people reunite with their lost, forgotten, or unclaimed money. Every year, billions of dollars in unclaimed funds sit in state treasuries, banks, and other financial institutions, waiting to be returned to their rightful owners.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to make it easy and free for everyone to search for and claim their unclaimed money. We believe that everyone deserves access to what's rightfully theirs, and we're committed to providing this service at no cost.

What We Do

Unclaimed Money Finder aggregates data from multiple sources to create a comprehensive, searchable database of unclaimed property. Our platform allows you to:

  • Search for unclaimed money using your name and location
  • View detailed information about potential matches
  • Get step-by-step instructions on how to claim your money
  • Access resources and links to official state agencies

Why Money Becomes Unclaimed

There are many reasons why money becomes unclaimed:

  • Moving: People move and don't update their address with all institutions
  • Name Changes: Marriage, divorce, or legal name changes can break the connection
  • Forgotten Accounts: Old bank accounts, utility deposits, or insurance policies are forgotten
  • Uncashed Checks: Paychecks, refunds, or rebates never get deposited
  • Lost Track: Stocks, bonds, or other investments lose contact with owners
  • Unknown Inheritance: Beneficiaries may not know they're entitled to funds

Our Commitment to You

We are committed to:

  • Remaining Free: We will never charge you to search or claim your money
  • Protecting Your Privacy: Your information is never sold or shared with third parties
  • Providing Accurate Information: We regularly update our database with the latest records

How We're Different

Unlike many services that charge fees or take a percentage of your claim, Unclaimed Money Finder is completely free. We don't believe in profiting from helping people claim what's already theirs. Our service is supported by our commitment to public good.

The Scale of the Problem

According to the National Association of Unclaimed Property Administrators (NAUPA):

  • More than $50 billion in unclaimed property is currently held by state treasuries
  • Approximately 1 in 7 people have unclaimed property waiting for them
  • The average claim value is between $800 and $1,500
  • States return billions of dollars to rightful owners each year
